4 · Where to click, site by site

Google (most important — this is what shows when people search)

  1. Go to business.google.com and sign in with the Google account that manages TJ’s. (Tip: if you’re signed in, you can also just Google “TJ’s Italian Cafe” and click “Edit profile.”)
  2. Click Edit profile → Hours and enter the hours above.
  3. To promote brunch: click Add update (or “Posts”) and paste the short blurb, ideally with a brunch photo.
  4. To show up in “breakfast” searches: in Edit profile → Business category, keep “Italian restaurant” as the main category and ADD “Breakfast restaurant” and “Brunch restaurant” as additional categories. Then under Attributes / More, tick “Serves breakfast” and “Serves brunch.” This is the single biggest thing that makes TJ’s appear when someone nearby searches “breakfast.” (Yelp and TripAdvisor have the same idea — add “Breakfast & Brunch” as a category there too.)

Yelp

  1. Go to biz.yelp.com and sign in.
  2. Click Business Information → edit Hours.
  3. Paste the short blurb into From the Business / Specialties.

TripAdvisor

  1. Go to tripadvisor.com/Owners and sign in to the Management Center.
  2. Choose Profile → Edit business details → update Hours.
  3. The short blurb fits nicely in the business description.

OpenTable

  1. Sign in at restaurant.opentable.com.
  2. Update hours under Restaurant settings / profile — if you don’t see the option, message their support from that same page and they’ll set it for you.

Facebook

  1. Open the TJ’s Facebook Page as an admin.
  2. Go to About → Contact & basic info → Hours and enter the hours above.
  3. Then make a regular post with the short blurb + a brunch photo — that’s your advertisement.

Instagram

  1. Post a brunch photo with the short blurb as the caption.
  2. Add “Weekend Brunch Sat & Sun 10–2” to the profile bio if there’s room.

Twitter / X

  1. Post the short blurb (it fits!) with a photo, and pin it to the profile.

That’s it! Once you’ve done a site, the change usually shows within minutes (Google can take a day). If any site gives you trouble, tell me what it said and I’ll help you figure out the next click. — Ava
